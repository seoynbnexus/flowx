Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FlowX ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect your Instagram or Facebook account through our platform.

Information We Collect

When you connect your social media account via OAuth, we collect the following information from your connected Facebook or Instagram account:

  • Public profile information — username, display name, profile picture
  • Instagram account type — whether your account is a Business or Creator account
  • Instagram Business Account ID — the unique identifier for your connected Instagram Business account
  • Follower count — the number of followers on your connected account
  • Facebook Pages — the list of Facebook Pages you manage (to verify your Instagram Business account linkage)
  • Access tokens — short-lived and long-lived authentication tokens required to maintain the connection

We do not collect private messages, email addresses from your social accounts, or any personal information beyond what is listed above.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Account verification — verifying that you own the connected Instagram Business or Creator account
  • Content publishing — enabling authorized content posting to your connected account (with your explicit consent)
  • Analytics — providing engagement insights and performance metrics for your connected accounts
  • Platform operations — maintaining the connection between your FlowX account and your social media account

Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously:

  • Encrypted tokens — all access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-CBC encryption
  • Secure transmission — all data transmitted between our servers and Meta's APIs uses HTTPS encryption
  • Limited access — only authorized personnel have access to your data, and only when necessary for platform operations
  • Token rotation — long-lived tokens are automatically refreshed and rotated to maintain security

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — as required for the OAuth authentication flow and Graph API operations
  • Legal compliance — when required by law or to protect our legal rights

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke access — disconnect your account at any time through your settings page or via Meta's Apps and Websites settings
  • Request deletion — request that we delete your connected account data from our systems
  • Access your data — request a copy of the information we hold about your connected account

Data Retention

We retain your connected account data and tokens for as long as your account remains connected. When you disconnect your account or request deletion, we remove all associated data, including access tokens and stored profile information, within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
